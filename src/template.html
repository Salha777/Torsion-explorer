How these surfaces were made

Level of theory. B3LYP-D3(BJ)/6-311G(d,p) with PCM water, Gaussian 16 Rev. A.03. Neutral DMT.

Surface. χ1 and χ2 frozen on a 10° grid, with every other coordinate optimised independently at each point. DMT is achiral, so half the grid was computed and the rest completed by mirror symmetry, (τ1, τ2) → (−τ1, −τ2).

Structures. Each grid point's structure is its final constrained optimisation; mirror-symmetry cells show the reflected structure of the computed point. The viewer snaps to the nearest grid point and blends visually between consecutive structures; neither the snapped structure nor the blend is a calculated geometry for the probe position itself.

Interpolation.A periodic bicubic spline passes through every grid energy and has continuous second derivatives, so the Hessian shown is defined everywhere. Between grid points it is an interpolation, not a calculation. Grid points that changed bonding on optimisation (steric clashes) are excluded and drawn as hatched cells.

Minima and free energies. Each unique minimum was fully re-optimised with frequencies (no imaginary modes). Free energies use the quasi-RRHO entropy of Grimme (100 cm⁻¹ cutoff) at 298.15 K; populations are Boltzmann weights over the minima.

Caveats. Electronic energies on the surfaces, free energies for the minima: the two are not directly comparable. The protonated amine, which binds the 5-HT₂ₐ receptor, has different preferred conformers; those surfaces are not shown here.

Load your own data

Paste a relaxed scan as three columns: τ1 (°), τ2 (°), energy. Commas, tabs or spaces all work; header lines are skipped. A complete regular grid is interpolated with the same periodic spline; anything else is fitted with a periodic Fourier series.
